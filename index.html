What does Recount check, and what can it not tell you?
  • Test statistics. t, F, χ², r and z results are turned back into a p-value from the statistic and degrees of freedom. Because the statistic is itself rounded, the p-value is a range. A reported p outside that range does not match. If the mismatch also flips significance at α, it is marked changes the conclusion.
  • GRIM test. A mean of whole-number scores from n people can only take certain values (k / n). If the reported mean is not one of them, something is off. It only works for whole-number data and small samples; set items per scale if the mean is of several items.
  • Percentages. “12/40 (35%)” is checked against 12 ÷ 40.
  • Limits. A match does not prove a result is right, and a mismatch is not proof of misconduct: typos, one-tailed tests, corrections (Welch, Greenhouse–Geisser) and rounding in software all cause them. It reads common APA-style patterns only.
Method after statcheck (Nuijten et al., 2016) and GRIM (Brown & Heathers, 2017). Distributions are implemented from scratch and verified against SciPy.
